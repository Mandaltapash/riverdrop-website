🌿 Our Ingredients

Every sip begins with the finest natural ingredients sourced directly from Indian farms. From sun-ripened mangoes to hand-picked rose petals, we bring you nature at its purest.

Nature First, Always

At RiverDrop, every bottle begins its journey long before it reaches our production floor. We work directly with over 15 partner farms spread across the heartlands of Kerala, Karnataka, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra — regions that have been nurturing the ingredients behind India's most beloved traditional beverages for centuries.

Our sourcing team visits each farm personally, ensuring the soil is healthy, the farming practices are sustainable, and the produce is harvested at peak ripeness. By cutting out middlemen entirely, we not only get fresher ingredients at better prices — we also build lasting relationships with the farmers who make RiverDrop possible.

We maintain an absolute zero-compromise policy on artificial additives. No artificial flavours, no synthetic colours, no chemical preservatives — ever. What you taste in every sip is exactly what nature intended, carefully preserved and crafted by our team of traditional recipe experts.

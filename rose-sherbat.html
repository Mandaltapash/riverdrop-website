Rose Sherbat

Floral elegance in every sip

₹40 / 250ml
★★★★☆ 4.7 (987 reviews)

Rose Sherbat is a living tribute to India's grandest culinary heritage — the Mughal royal courts, where perfumed rose petals were steeped in cool water and served to emperors seeking respite from the heat. Our recipe honours that legacy with real rose petal extract and hand-prepared gulkand, creating a drink of unmatched floral depth that is at once romantic, refreshing and deeply cooling. Close your eyes, take a sip, and let the delicate scent of a thousand blossoms transport you to another era.

Ingredients
  • 🌿 Real Rose Petals Extract
  • 🌿 Rose Water
  • 🌿 Gulkand (Rose Petal Jam)
  • 🌿 Cardamom
  • 🌿 Kewra Water

5 Ways to Use Aam Panna Beyond Just Drinking It

Aam Panna is India's quintessential summer cooler, but its tangy, roasted-cumin-spiced profile makes it a secret weapon in the kitchen too. Use it as a vibrant marinade for grilled paneer and chicken, swirl it into sweet-and-sour mint chutneys, or freeze it into refreshing popsicles for hot afternoons. Once you start experimenting, a single bottle will never stay in your fridge for long.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

About Our Products

Are RiverDrop drinks 100% natural?
Yes, every single ingredient in our drinks is 100% natural. We use no artificial flavours, colours, or preservatives. Our recipes are inspired by traditional Indian formulations and have been crafted with just the ingredients nature intended.
Do your drinks contain sugar?
Our drinks are sweetened with natural sweeteners like jaggery, cane sugar, or fruit sugars — never refined white sugar or artificial sweeteners. The sweetness levels are carefully calibrated to match traditional recipes.
Are RiverDrop drinks suitable for children?
Yes! Our drinks are made from natural ingredients and are safe for children above 2 years of age. However, Thandai contains nut-based ingredients, so please check for allergies before giving it to children.
Do you have any drinks for diabetics?
Jaljeera and Kanji are our lowest sugar options and may be suitable for diabetics in moderation. However, we recommend consulting your doctor before including any sweetened beverage in a diabetic diet.

Storage & Shelf Life

How long do the drinks last?
Unopened, our sealed bottles last 6-9 months from the manufacturing date. Once opened, consume within 2-3 days. Kanji, being a fermented product, should be consumed within 7 days of opening and kept refrigerated.
Do I need to refrigerate the drinks?
Unopened bottles can be stored at room temperature, away from direct sunlight. After opening, always refrigerate and consume quickly. Kanji must be refrigerated at all times.
Why does my drink look slightly different each batch?
Because we use 100% natural ingredients, there can be slight variations in colour and flavour between batches — just like home cooking! This is a sign of authenticity, not a defect. The taste and quality remain consistently excellent.

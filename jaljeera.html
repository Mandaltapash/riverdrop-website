Jaljeera

Tangy digestive delight

₹30 / 250ml
★★★★★ 4.8 (1,876 reviews)

Jaljeera is the ancient Indian answer to digestive wellness — a tangy, spiced elixir built around the powerhouse ingredient jeera (cumin), celebrated in Ayurveda for centuries as a natural digestive aid. Our recipe layers roasted cumin's earthy warmth with the puckering zing of dried mango powder and the brightness of fresh lemon, creating a drink that is as therapeutic as it is thirst-quenching. Whether sipped after a hearty meal or poured over crushed ice on a sweltering afternoon, Jaljeera is the refreshing reset your body craves.

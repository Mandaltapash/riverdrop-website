Thandai

The festive drink of Holi

₹45 / 250ml
★★★★★ 4.9 (3,210 reviews)

Thandai is India's most beloved festive beverage, inseparable from the joyous celebration of Holi and Mahashivratri across the country. Crafted from a royal blend of almonds, melon seeds, poppy seeds, rose petals, and aromatic spices steeped in creamy milk, it is both deeply cooling and intensely nourishing. RiverDrop's Thandai honours this centuries-old recipe from the lanes of Varanasi and Jaipur, delivering every sip with the warmth of tradition and the exuberance of festival.

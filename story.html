The Story Behind Every Sip

We didn't invent these flavours. They were always there — in grandmothers' kitchens, summer afternoons, and childhood memories. We just bottled them for you.

Where It All Began

A kitchen in Thrissur. A childhood summer. A lifetime of flavour.

🏠

Every great story begins somewhere small and deeply personal. For Arjun Nair, it began in his grandmother Kamakshi Amma's kitchen in Thrissur, Kerala — a narrow, sunlit room that smelled perpetually of raw mango, cardamom, and fresh mint. Each summer, as the Kerala heat rolled in thick and unforgiving, Kamakshi Amma would line the kitchen counter with clay pots of Aam Panna, rose petal sherbat, and tangy jaljeera — drinks that felt, in Arjun's words, "like the whole of India in a single sip."

It was these summers that never left Arjun. Through his years in culinary school, through eight years of research into traditional Indian beverage recipes, he kept returning to those flavours. He would call his grandmother for recipes — always too vague, always "a handful of this, a little of that" — and slowly, patiently, he learned to translate memory into measure. Each recipe went through dozens of iterations, tested on family, friends, and eventually on Chennai's discerning palates at a small farmers' market stall in 2009.

In 2010, alongside his college friend Priya Menon, Arjun launched RiverDrop from a rented kitchen space in Chennai — just 200 square feet, one cold press machine, and a dream. Their first product was Aam Panna. The first batch sold out in two hours. The second batch sold out before noon. They knew then that they weren't just selling a drink. They were selling a feeling — the irreplaceable warmth of tradition, the nostalgia of an Indian summer, the reassurance of something pure in a world full of artificial substitutes. That feeling is what RiverDrop has bottled, batch after careful batch, ever since.

Our Journey

From a home kitchen to 15 states and counting

2010
The Beginning 🌱
Started in a home kitchen in Thrissur with just one product — Aam Panna. The first batch of 50 bottles sold out at a Chennai farmers' market in under two hours.
2012
First Retail Listing 🛒
RiverDrop secured its first listing in Chennai supermarkets with 3 flavours: Aam Panna, Jaljeera, and Nimbu Pani. A milestone that proved the brand was ready for the mainstream.
2015
Expanding Horizons 🗺️
Expanded to 8 cities across South and West India. Launched Rose Sherbat and Kokum, cementing RiverDrop's reputation for bringing regional flavours to a national audience.
2019
1 Lakh Bottles & Holi Special 🎉
Reached the milestone of 1 lakh bottles sold. Launched Thandai as a limited-edition Holi special — it sold out within days and became a permanent fan favourite.
2024
A National Brand 🇮🇳
10+ flavours. 15 states. 50,000+ happy customers. RiverDrop is now a beloved household name across India — and we're only just getting started.

What Makes Us Different

Our commitments that set every RiverDrop bottle apart

Our ingredients come directly from farmers we know by name. No middlemen, no compromises. The raw mangoes in your Aam Panna were harvested by the same families we have worked with since 2011.

Every recipe is tested more than 100 times before it leaves our kitchen. We tweak, adjust, refine, and repeat until it tastes exactly the way tradition intended — not a shortcut in sight.

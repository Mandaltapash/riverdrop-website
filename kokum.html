Kokum

Coastal cool, the Konkan way

₹35 / 250ml
★★★★★ 4.9 (1,543 reviews)

Kokum is a sour, vibrant fruit native to the Konkan coast of India, celebrated for its deep crimson hue and intensely tangy flavour. Our RiverDrop Kokum drink is the ultimate cooling companion for tropical Indian summers, delivering refreshing tartness with a natural sweetness from jaggery. This beloved beverage has been a staple in Goan and Maharashtrian households for centuries, served chilled as a digestive and heat-beater after long afternoons.

  • Black Salt
  • Roasted Cumin
  • Jaggery

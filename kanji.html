Kanji

Probiotic fermented goodness

₹38 / 250ml
★★★★☆ 4.6 (412 reviews)

Kanji is a cherished North Indian fermented drink traditionally prepared around Holi using heirloom black carrots and crushed mustard seeds. Slow-fermented in earthenware jars for three days, it bursts with a bold, tangy, and pleasantly sour flavour that awakens the palate. Packed with live probiotic cultures, antioxidants, and natural minerals, Kanji offers deep nutritional value and time-tested digestive support in every vibrant crimson glass.

🌿 Ingredients
  • Black Carrots
  • Mustard Seeds (Rai)
  • Black Salt
  • Red Chilli Powder
  • Water (fermented 3 days)
